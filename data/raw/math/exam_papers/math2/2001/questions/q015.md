# 2001 数学二第 15
## 题目

Given $f'(x)=g(x)$, $g'(x)=2e^x-f(x)$, and $f(0)=0, g(0)=2$, compute

$$
\int_0^\pi\left[\frac{g(x)}{1+x}-\frac{f(x)}{(1+x)^2}\right]dx.
$$

## 标准答案

$\dfrac{e^{\pi}+1}{1+\pi}$

## 解析

Eliminate $g$ to obtain $f''+f=2e^x$. After solving for $f$, notice that the integrand is $\left(\frac{f(x)}{1+x}\right)'$.
