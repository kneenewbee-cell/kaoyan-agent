# 2001 数学二第 18
## 题目

Let $f(x)$ have a continuous second derivative on $[-a,a]$ with $a>0$ and $f(0)=0$.

(1) Write the first-order Maclaurin formula with Lagrange remainder.

(2) Prove that there exists $\eta\in[-a,a]$ such that

$$
a^3f''(\eta)=3\int_{-a}^{a}f(x)\,dx.
$$

## 标准答案

$(1)\ f(x)=f(0)+f'(0)x+\dfrac12f''(\xi)x^2;\quad (2)\ see explanation.$

## 解析

Use the first-order Maclaurin expansion with Lagrange remainder and integrate it on $[-a,a]$. Then apply the intermediate value theorem to the continuous function $f''$.
