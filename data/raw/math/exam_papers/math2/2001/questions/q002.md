# 2001 数学二第 2
## 题目

Let $y=f(x)$ be defined by

$$
e^{2x+y}-\cos(xy)=e-1.
$$

Find the normal line at $(0,1)$.

## 标准答案

$x-2y+2=0$

## 解析

Differentiate implicitly:

$$
e^{2x+y}(2+y')+\sin(xy)(xy'+y)=0.
$$

At $(0,1)$ we get $y'=-2$, so the normal slope is $1/2$.
