# 2001 数学二第 4
## 题目

Find the curve through $\left(\dfrac12,0\right)$ satisfying

$$
y'\arcsin x+\frac{y}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}=1.
$$

## 标准答案

$y=x\arcsin x-\dfrac12$

## 解析

Recognize the left-hand side as $\dfrac{d}{dx}(y\arcsin x)$, integrate once, and use the given point.
