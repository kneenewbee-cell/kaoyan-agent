# 2001 数学二第 11
## 题目

Compute

$$
\int\frac{dx}{(2x^2+1)\sqrt{x^2+1}}.
$$

## 标准答案

$\arctan\left(\dfrac{x}{\sqrt{x^2+1}}\right)+C$

## 解析

Set $x=\tan u$. Then the integral becomes $\int \frac{\cos u}{1+\sin^2u}du$. Let $z=\sin u$.
