# 2001 数学二第 8
## 题目

How many inflection points does the curve $y=(x-1)^2(x-3)^2$ have?

A. $0$  B. $1$  C. $2$  D. $3$

## 标准答案

C

## 解析

Compute $y''=4(3x^2-12x+11)$. It has two distinct real zeros and changes sign at both.
