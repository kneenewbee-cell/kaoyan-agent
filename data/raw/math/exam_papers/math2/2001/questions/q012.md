# 2001 数学二第 12
## 题目

Let

$$
f(x)=\lim_{t\to x}\left(\frac{\sin t}{\sin x}\right)^{\frac{x}{t-\sin x}}.
$$

Find $f(x)$ and classify its discontinuities.

## 标准答案

$f(x)=e^{x/\sin x}$; $x=0$ is removable, and $x=k\pi\ (k\ne0)$ are infinite discontinuities.

## 解析

Take logarithms and use $\ln(1+u)\sim u$. The exponent limit is $x/\sin x$.
