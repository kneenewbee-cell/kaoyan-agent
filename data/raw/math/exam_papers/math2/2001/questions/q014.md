# 2001 数学二第 14
## 题目

Let $f(x)$ be differentiable on $[0,+\infty)$ with $f(0)=0$, and let $g$ be its inverse. If

$$
\int_0^{f(x)}g(t)\,dt=x^2e^x,
$$

find $f(x)$.

## 标准答案

$f(x)=e^x(x+1)-1$

## 解析

Differentiate both sides and use $g(f(x))=x$ to get $f'(x)=(x+2)e^x$, then integrate and use $f(0)=0$.
