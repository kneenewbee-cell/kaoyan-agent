## 详细解析

### 第 1 题

- 答案：$-\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{6}$

Rationalize the numerator and cancel $(x-1)$:

$$
\sqrt{3-x}-\sqrt{1+x}=\frac{-2(x-1)}{\sqrt{3-x}+\sqrt{1+x}}.
$$

Then substitute $x=1$.

### 第 2 题

- 答案：$x-2y+2=0$

Differentiate implicitly:

$$
e^{2x+y}(2+y')+\sin(xy)(xy'+y)=0.
$$

At $(0,1)$ we get $y'=-2$, so the normal slope is $1/2$.

### 第 3 题

- 答案：$\dfrac{\pi}{8}$

The term $x^3\cos^2x$ is odd, so its integral on the symmetric interval is $0$.
Reduce the rest to $2\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin^2x\cos^2x\,dx$.

### 第 4 题

- 答案：$y=x\arcsin x-\dfrac12$

Recognize the left-hand side as $\dfrac{d}{dx}(y\arcsin x)$, integrate once, and use the given point.

### 第 5 题

- 答案：$-2$

The system has infinitely many solutions iff the coefficient matrix and augmented matrix have the same rank, smaller than $3$.
Row reduction gives $a=-2$.

### 第 6 题

- 答案：B

The values of $f$ are only $0$ and $1$, and both satisfy $f(0)=f(1)=1$.

### 第 7 题

- 答案：B

Use $(1-\cos x)\ln(1+x^2)\sim \tfrac12x^4$, $x\sin x^n\sim x^{n+1}$, and $e^{x^2}-1\sim x^2$.

### 第 8 题

- 答案：C

Compute $y''=4(3x^2-12x+11)$. It has two distinct real zeros and changes sign at both.

### 第 9 题

- 答案：A

Let $F(x)=f(x)-x$. Then $F(1)=F'(1)=0$ and $F'(x)=f'(x)-1$. Since $f'$ is strictly decreasing, $F$ has a local maximum at $x=1$.

### 第 10 题

- 答案：D

Read the sign changes of $f'(x)$ from the monotonicity of the given graph. Only option D matches.

### 第 11 题

- 答案：$\arctan\left(\dfrac{x}{\sqrt{x^2+1}}\right)+C$

Set $x=\tan u$. Then the integral becomes $\int \frac{\cos u}{1+\sin^2u}du$. Let $z=\sin u$.

### 第 12 题

- 答案：$f(x)=e^{x/\sin x}$; $x=0$ is removable, and $x=k\pi\ (k\ne0)$ are infinite discontinuities.

Take logarithms and use $\ln(1+u)\sim u$. The exponent limit is $x/\sin x$.

### 第 13 题

- 答案：$9$

Compute $y'$ and $y''$, then use the curvature-radius formula and the arc-length differential.

### 第 14 题

- 答案：$f(x)=e^x(x+1)-1$

Differentiate both sides and use $g(f(x))=x$ to get $f'(x)=(x+2)e^x$, then integrate and use $f(0)=0$.

### 第 15 题

- 答案：$\dfrac{e^{\pi}+1}{1+\pi}$

Eliminate $g$ to obtain $f''+f=2e^x$. After solving for $f$, notice that the integrand is $\left(\frac{f(x)}{1+x}\right)'$.

### 第 16 题

- 答案：$(1)\ y=\dfrac14-x^2;\qquad (2)\ Y=-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3}X+\dfrac13.$

The condition gives the differential equation $y-xy'=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$. Solve it and use the given point. Then optimize the enclosed area over the tangent point.

### 第 17 题

- 答案：$6$ hours

With $V=\frac23\pi r^3$ and $S=2\pi r^2$, the law $dV/dt=-kS$ simplifies to $dr/dt=-k$, so $r$ decreases linearly. The given volume condition implies the radius is halved after $3$ hours.

### 第 18 题

- 答案：$(1)\ f(x)=f(0)+f'(0)x+\dfrac12f''(\xi)x^2;\quad (2)\ see explanation.$

Use the first-order Maclaurin expansion with Lagrange remainder and integrate it on $[-a,a]$. Then apply the intermediate value theorem to the continuous function $f''$.

### 第 19 题

- 答案：$X=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&5\\0&1&2\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}$

Rearrange the equation into $(A-B)X(A-B)=E$, so $X=(A-B)^{-2}$.

### 第 20 题

- 答案：$t\ne1,\,-1$

The $\beta_i$ remain solutions automatically. They form another basis exactly when they are linearly independent. The coefficient matrix relative to the $\alpha_i$ basis has determinant $1-t^4$.
