# 1988 数学三第 19 题

## 题目

已知向量组$\alpha_1,\alpha_2, \cdots ,\alpha_s$ ($s \ge 2$)线性无关．设
$$\beta_1 = \alpha_1 + \alpha_2, \beta_2 = \alpha_2 + \alpha_3, \cdots,
\beta_{s - 1} = \alpha_{s - 1} + \alpha_s, \beta_s = \alpha_s + \alpha_1.$$
试讨论向量组$\beta_1,\beta_2, \cdots ,\beta_s$的线性相关性．
